# 🦸 Heroes

## Pakarat Rakab

### Gorka & Morka

* A magical Poleaxe that can be thrown.

### Orc's Growl

* Slows and de-buffs enemies ATK in a range.

### Poleaxe Dash

* Dash forward, dealing DMG and stunning the first enemy hit.

### Poleaxe Spin

* Spin your poleaxe, dealing DMG and knocking enemies back.

### Anger Issues

* Taking continuous DMG makes you briefly become Berserk.  

### Berserk

* Increases ATK, Crit Chance and Movement SPD.
